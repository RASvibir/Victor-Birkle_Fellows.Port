Motivation

Why AI safety

Most of my production time around conversational systems was spent on the boring failure modes: bad data, silent drift, and confidence that stayed high while quality slipped. That is not abstract risk — it is what happens when a system keeps looking fine while it stops being reliable.

I care about empirical AI safety because I have already had to diagnose those gaps under schedule pressure, and I want the next stretch of work to be about measuring and reducing them carefully — especially where high-confidence errors compound into real-world harm.
